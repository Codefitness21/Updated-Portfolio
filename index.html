Health Meets Software

After walking on to the Old Dominion Division I basketball team (first walk on in 10 years), and completing my undergraduate degree in psychology, I began a career in personal training. Simultaneously, I earned a Masters Degree in Nutrition and completed my dietetic internship. Obtaining my degree required completion of a course to build a first rate blog in WordPress, which reignited my interest in learning software development.

After obtaining my Masters, and while working in training and nutrition I observed an opportunity to better serve my clients and improve their experience by simplifying their health tracking apps. I carried this problem solving mindset into my software development education and career, where the apps designed for users yeild a product that's easy to use and navigate, thus delivering value and a healthy experience.

When I'm not building products, I enjoy weight lifting in my garage gym, training in Taekwondo, and sprinting on Sunday mornings with my sprint club. I consistently practice meditation and journaling as they are central to my overall well-being. But my "ROCKS" are my family and closest friends who are foundational to keeping me centered.
